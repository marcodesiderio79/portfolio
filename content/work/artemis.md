## Problem
> Investors scan for specific data points, not long content.

Artemis, a boutique asset manager with a strong reputation among professional investors, had a website that behaved like a marketing site rather than a product.

Fund data was updated by hand, the site was hard to scale, and it didn't match how professional investors actually work. Inconsistent data also chips away at trust, which is a bad look for a fund manager.

## Approach
**Design for the real workflow.** Discover, evaluate, validate: find the right fund, compare performance and risk, then download documents and share with clients. Every page was mapped to one of those steps.

**Fund pages as hubs.** Funds, performance and documents come first, in structured, scannable sections.

**Automated data.** I worked with engineering to connect fund data and document updates automatically, so nobody retypes a number.

## System
**Reusable components.** A modular content system lets the internal team build new pages on their own, without breaking consistency. A reusable component system for flexible pages, with data that updates itself.

## Outcome
I redesigned it into a data-led platform: fund pages as information hubs, automated fund data and documents, and a reusable component system so the team can publish new pages without waiting for design or development.
