Quick intro

The short version

I spent my first years in advertising in São Paulo. Then I moved to London and started over as a designer, in my second language. Today I build design systems for UK asset managers, where the data is complex and the regulation is strict.

"I design digital products that make people's lives a little less complicated."

More about me

One solid system can carry many brands and complex projects. I know, because I keep asking it to.

  • 15+ years in design and advertising. Almost 10 in product design, after 6 as an Account Director in São Paulo.
  • 3 years designing for UK asset managers, including Royal London, J O Hambro and Artemis.
  • 10+ clients on one design system. Built once for the betting industry. Ten brands, one system, far fewer arguments about button colours.
  • 40+ reusable components which is how we delivered Royal London in 3 months.
